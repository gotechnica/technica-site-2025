About

Welcome to our Techni-Fellows Wonderland! Our Technica Fellowship Program is an 8-week initiative where exceptional rising college freshmen will be given hands-on experience working on a real-world technology project. This year’s cohort was tasked with creating a themed website of their own design. So, come embark on a journey through the minds of our fellows and see what they have made!

Inspiration

Before diving into their projects, fellows crafted mood boards. These visual compilations served as inspiration, helping fellows to refine their ideas and set the tone for their websites. By exploring different themes, color palettes, and design elements, they were able to establish a clear vision for their projects before moving on to development.
